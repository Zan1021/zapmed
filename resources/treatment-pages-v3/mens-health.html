A quick, confidential medical questionnaire. You can start right away. No account is needed first.

Before your visit

Your questions, answered.

Will anyone see my answers?

Only your care team. Your answers are confidential.

Do I need an account first?

No. You go straight into the questionnaire and create your account later, when you choose a time to see your doctor.

Does a consultation include medication?

A prescription depends on your doctor's assessment. Medication is billed separately and you can discuss the cost before anything is dispensed.
