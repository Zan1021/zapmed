What to expect.

What working with Zapmed looks like, from eligibility to ongoing care.

01 Complete a health assessment

A quick, confidential medical questionnaire, then book a convenient online consultation with one of our doctors for R700.

02 Meet your doctor by video

Discuss whether medication is right for you. Your doctor will listen to your full picture and what you’ve already tried, and work out a plan that fits you. Meet our doctors.

03 Your plan is built around you

If prescribed, your SAHPRA-approved medication is dispatched from a licensed South African pharmacy for FREE discreet delivery. Your doctor will walk you through the cost before anything is dispensed. If your doctor asks for blood tests, you pay the lab directly.

04 Ongoing health coach support

For R450 per month a registered dietitian walks with you the whole way from taking the medication, to check ins via WhatsApp about nutrition, side-effect management, motivation and real-life adjustments. Medication is delivered to your door FREE each month. Medication costs are charged separately depending on your recommended dose.

05 Community support and exclusive benefits

As a member you will gain access to useful topics and partner benefits to support you throughout your lifestyle changes.

Before your visit

Your questions, answered.

How should I prepare for my consultation?

Have your medication list and any recent blood results handy. You can attend without test results and discuss next steps with the doctor. For help arranging tests or sharing existing results, contact doctors@zapmed.co.za.

Will I need blood tests?

Your doctor decides based on your history and the limits of a video examination. They may request blood sugar, kidney, liver, thyroid, cholesterol or blood count tests. In selected cases, they may also request amylase or lipase.

Does a consultation include medication?

Medication is billed separately. A prescription depends on your doctor’s assessment. You can discuss costs before anything is dispensed.

What support is available between visits?

Membership includes dietitian-led coaching, messaging and guidance on nutrition, movement and maintaining progress. The once-off option covers a single consultation.
